MouseWorks Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

MouseWorks is a Mac app that changes how your mouse scrolls and what its buttons do. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: MouseWorks collects nothing.

What MouseWorks collects

Nothing. MouseWorks has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet. The developer never receives any information about you or how you use the app.

What MouseWorks reads on your Mac

To do its job, MouseWorks uses two macOS permissions that you grant:

  • Input Monitoring lets MouseWorks see mouse events: scrolling, mouse buttons and mouse movement while a button is held. It never reads your keyboard. When you record a keyboard shortcut for a mouse button, MouseWorks only sees the keys you press while its own Settings window is in front.
  • Accessibility lets MouseWorks change those mouse events and send its own, for example smooth scrolling or the shortcut a button is mapped to.

These events are handled as they happen and then forgotten. MouseWorks doesn't record, store or send them anywhere.

Your settings

Your MouseWorks settings, such as scrolling preferences, button mappings and per-app settings, are stored on your Mac. If "Sync settings with iCloud" is turned on, they're also kept in your own iCloud account, so they can be the same on your other Macs. This uses Apple's iCloud, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it. The developer can't access your iCloud data. You can turn sync off at any time in MouseWorks ▸ Settings ▸ General.

Children

MouseWorks doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
