MouseWorks Support

Need help with MouseWorks? Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, email ryanleesoftware@proton.me and include your Mac model, your macOS version, your mouse model and the MouseWorks version (MouseWorks ▸ About MouseWorks).

Getting started

MouseWorks doesn't do anything. MouseWorks needs two permissions. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and make sure MouseWorks is turned on under both Input Monitoring and Accessibility. Then open MouseWorks ▸ Settings ▸ Permissions. If both are granted but it still doesn't work, click Relaunch MouseWorks there.

Where is MouseWorks? It lives in the menu bar (the mouse icon). If you've hidden that icon, open MouseWorks again from your Applications folder to get to Settings.

My trackpad scrolling didn't change. That's on purpose. MouseWorks only changes mice with a scroll wheel. Trackpads and the Magic Mouse are left exactly as they are.

Scrolling

My mouse scrolls the wrong way. Turn on Settings ▸ Scrolling ▸ Reverse vertical scrolling. It only changes the mouse, so your trackpad keeps its own direction.

Scrolling feels too slow or too fast. Change Distance per notch in Settings ▸ Scrolling. macOS's own scrolling speed (System Settings ▸ Mouse) still applies as well.

Buttons

The side buttons don't go back and forward in an app. Check that Universal back/forward is on in Settings ▸ Buttons. If one app still doesn't respond, add it in Settings ▸ Apps, open it with ›, turn off "Use MouseWorks' choice" and record the app's own back and forward shortcuts.

The extra buttons on top of my mouse don't show up. On many mice these buttons change the DPI inside the mouse itself and never send anything to the Mac, so no app can remap them.

A button mapping does nothing. If the button is also your drag-to-scroll button, it only runs Click and Double-click mappings. Choose a different drag button in Settings ▸ Scrolling ▸ Drag to Scroll, or turn it off.

A button sometimes clicks twice. Turn on Ignore accidental double clicks in Settings ▸ Buttons. It's made for worn mouse switches.

Games and other apps

How do I turn MouseWorks off in one app? Settings ▸ Apps ▸ +, choose the app, open it with › and turn on Turn off MouseWorks in this app.

Can I use MouseWorks with another mouse utility? It's best not to run two at once. Apps such as Logi Options+, Mos, LinearMouse or Mac Mouse Fix change the same events, and they'll fight over them.

Privacy

MouseWorks collects no data and never reads your keyboard. See the privacy policy for details.
